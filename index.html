Welcome to Kuvuki

A beautiful space to slow down, unwind and reconnect.

Kuvuki Beach Lodge is a serene beach lodge on the shores of Langma Village, just west of Kokrobite. It sits above the palms with floor-to-ceiling windows that frame the Atlantic. Every suite is designed around rest — soft warm lighting, plush bedding, spa-style bathrooms and a view you'll keep coming back to.

Whether it's a weekend escape, an anniversary or the moment you finally ask that big question, we're here to make it unforgettable.

Good to know

Frequently asked

Is breakfast included?

Yes — our rates are Bed & Breakfast, so breakfast is included with every stay.

How much does a night cost?

Rooms range from GHS 1,000 to GHS 2,000 per night, Bed & Breakfast. The exact rate depends on the room and your dates, so send us a request and we'll confirm it.

Can you help me plan a proposal or anniversary?

Absolutely. Romantic beach dinners and proposals start from GHS 960 and are strictly by reservation. We also decorate for birthdays, anniversaries and weddings. Tell us your vision in the booking form.

How do I confirm my booking?

Send a request through the form above, or call/WhatsApp 020 041 8854. We'll reply with availability and payment details to secure your dates.

Where exactly are you?

We're in Langma Village, just west of Kokrobite on the coast west of Accra. WhatsApp us and we'll share a pin and directions.
