Welcome to Kuvuki

A beautiful space to slow down, unwind and reconnect.

Kuvuki Beach Lodge sits above the palms of Kokrobite, with floor-to-ceiling windows that frame the Atlantic. Every suite is designed around rest — soft warm lighting, plush bedding, spa-style bathrooms and a view you'll keep coming back to.

Whether it's a weekend escape, an anniversary or the moment you finally ask that big question, we're here to make it unforgettable.

Good to know

Frequently asked

Is breakfast included?

Yes — our rates are Bed & Breakfast, so breakfast is included with every stay.

How much does a night cost?

Suites start from GHS 2,000 per night. Rates can vary by suite, dates and package, so send us a request and we'll confirm the exact price.

Can you help me plan a proposal or anniversary?

Absolutely. Our proposal and "Better Together" packages include floral arches, neon signage, candles and a dedicated setup. Tell us your vision in the booking form.

How do I confirm my booking?

Send a request through the form above. We'll reply with availability and payment details to secure your dates.

Where exactly are you?

We're in Kokrobite, on the coast west of Accra. Message us and we'll share a pin and directions.
